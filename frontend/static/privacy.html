Privacy Policy

Lattice · Effective 22 November 2026

Lattice turns a topic you type into an explainer video. This page says what the service keeps about you and why. It is run by an individual developer as an early-stage project, not a company.

What we collect

  • Your Google account details — your email address, display name and profile picture URL. They come from Google through our sign-in provider, Supabase. We never see your Google password.
  • What you make — the topics and messages you type, the storyboards, scripts and videos generated from them, and your edits.
  • Usage records — when you use the service, which actions you take, how many model calls they used and what those cost. These enforce the daily limits.
  • Technical details — your IP address and browser type, kept with your account record and in server logs, to run and protect the service.

How we use it

To sign you in, to build and show your videos, to apply daily usage limits, and to keep the service secure and working. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it to train models.

Who else handles it

  • Google and Supabase handle sign-in.
  • An AI model provider (we send the text you type, and text derived from it, to a hosted language model through OpenRouter) to write storyboards and scripts. Do not type anything you would not want sent to such a provider.
  • Our hosting provider, where the server and your videos are stored.

Cookies

Only the cookies needed to keep you signed in (secure, HTTP-only session cookies) and a short-lived cookie used during the sign-in handshake. No advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

Your account record, videos and usage records are kept until you ask us to delete them. Usage records may be kept in aggregate afterwards.

Your choices

You can ask to see or delete your data, or your account, at any time by emailing {{CONTACT}}. Signing out ends your session on that browser.

Children

Lattice is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
